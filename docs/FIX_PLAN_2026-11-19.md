# IdeaToVideo fix plan, 2026-11-19

Written after a real, time-critical run on 2026-11-18: Zoltan needed one 1-minute video for a class in under an hour and the app could not deliver it. Claude then made two good videos outside the app with plain code (Python, Pillow, ffmpeg) in about 5 minutes each. The app must be able to do the same. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `STATUS.md` and `docs/BUILD_PLAN.md` first, add this as the next phase in `BUILD_PLAN.md`, log every choice in `DECISIONS.md`, update `STATUS.md` at the end. All existing rules stay (clean room, no GitHub Actions, no PR, local build, cross-platform, reliability layer), except where this plan changes one on purpose and says so.

Order of work: 1, 2, 3, 4 are the must-haves, in this order. 5 to 9 after them.

---

## 1. Identity: naming, creating, the top bar

What went wrong:
- A long Identity name pushes the dropdown over Home, ONE Video and the other menu items, so they cannot be clicked. Zoltan had to rename Identities to short names to use the app.
- The Identity stayed "New Identity" in places after renaming. A yellow bar said the folder keeps its name while a job runs.
- Creating a new Identity was unclear: Add Identity, Duplicate and the auto-made "New Identity" were mixed up. It took several tries an hour before a class.

What to build:
- **Top bar layout:** logo, then the full menu (Home, ONE Video, Identity, Wizards, Playlist, Publishing, Settings), then on the right the Identity switcher and the job status. The switcher has a fixed maximum width; a long name is cut with an ellipsis and shown in full in a tooltip and in the open list. Nothing in the bar may ever overlap another item, at any window width down to 1280 px. Add a UI test that renders the bar with a 60-character name and checks that every menu item is clickable.
- **Display name is separate from the folder:** every Identity gets a stable id (the folder name, never changed after creation) and a display name stored in its own settings file. Renaming changes only the display name, so it works at any time, also while a job runs. Remove the "folder keeps its name" banner. Migrate existing Identities: keep their folders, copy the current name into the display name.
- **One clear "New Identity" flow:** a single button "New Identity" (in the switcher list and on Identity, Manage Identity) opens one dialog: Name, Content language, and "Start from: Empty / Copy settings of <Identity>". Create switches to it and opens its recipe. No Identity is ever created automatically with the name "New Identity".
- Duplicate and Delete stay on Manage Identity, with plain labels.

## 2. Shorts (9:16)

Zoltan's decision changes (2026-11-18): IdeaToVideo makes short-form videos too, not only 16:9. Update the rule "16:9 only. No shorts." in `CLAUDE.md` and `BUILD_PLAN.md`, and log it in `DECISIONS.md`. "Never publish automatically" stays.

What to build:
- A **Format** choice in the recipe and on each video: Long form 16:9 (1920x1080) or Short 9:16 (1080x1920). The video can override the recipe.
- Every style preset (Motion Frames, 3D Explainer, Stick World, Archive Collage, Night Case File, Soft Clay) gets a 9:16 layout: bigger text, one idea per screen, safe areas for the platform buttons (keep the lower 20 percent and right 12 percent free of important content), captions in the middle third.
- Short length presets: 15, 30, 45, 60, 90 seconds.
- The avatar in 9:16: still HeyGen `avatar_iii`, generated at 9:16 framing at generation time, composited 1:1 as it comes (the existing avatar rule stays).
- The Publishing Card gets a short-form variant: one title line, a short description, hashtags. PDT export stays for long form.

## 3. Khmer, and three separate languages per video

What went wrong:
- Khmer is not in the language list.
- On 2026-11-18 a Khmer voiceover was made with ElevenLabs `eleven_v3` and Zoltan's clone. A Khmer speaker said it sounded Vietnamese. The reason: ElevenLabs text to speech does not support Khmer. The ElevenLabs language pages list 74 languages for Eleven v3 and 90+ for Eleven v4, and Khmer is in neither list (checked 2026-11-18). ElevenLabs speech to text (Scribe) does support Khmer.
- Zoltan did make a good Khmer video earlier with HeyGen (his voice clone and avatar). Check in the HeyGen API which voices and languages support Khmer before relying on it.

What to build: three language settings per video, each defaulting to the Identity's content language, each changeable on the video:
- **Voice language:** what the narrator speaks.
- **Caption language:** the burned-in captions or subtitles.
- **On-screen language:** the text inside the scenes (titles, labels, numbers, icons' words).

How it works:
- The script is written once in one language (the source language). Any of the three that differs from it gets a translation, made with `chat()` (the existing AI chain), shown on the Script tab for review and editing, sentence by sentence, before the voice is made. With no AI available, the user can paste the translation by hand.
- Captions in another language than the voice use the voice's sentence timings: each translated sentence is shown while its source sentence is spoken.
- **Capability check per language:** a table in the runner says, for each provider and model, which voice languages it supports (ElevenLabs per model, HeyGen, Kokoro, any other). The voice picker shows only voices that can speak the chosen voice language, and says plainly when none of the user's own voices can ("ElevenLabs cannot speak Khmer. HeyGen can, with your HeyGen voice."). Never send a language to a model that does not list it.
- Add Khmer (`km`) to every language list. Bundle **Noto Sans Khmer** (OFL) for captions and on-screen text, and check that Khmer renders correctly shaped in the HyperFrames output (a render test with a Khmer caption).
- A Short or long video for a Cambodian class is the reference case: voice Khmer (HeyGen) or English, captions Khmer, on-screen text English or Khmer.

## 4. A stuck AI call must never block a video

What went wrong: the scene plan with Gemini 3.5 Flash showed "The AI model is busy, switching to the other model" and ran for more than 10 minutes. The job stayed in "Jobs: 1 running" and blocked the next video.

What to build:
- Every AI step has a hard time limit (90 seconds for a scene plan, configurable). On time-out: try the next saved key once; if that also fails or times out, fall back automatically to the rule-made plan and add a note on the Preview tab. Never wait longer than about 3 minutes in total for one AI step.
- Stop always ends the job within a few seconds and frees the queue.
- The "Autopilot stopped" banner disappears as soon as the user continues by hand.

## 5. The free, rule-made scene plan must look good

What went wrong: "Simple plan without AI" gave text on a plain background, no figures, no icons, some on-screen text cut in half ("Finally we show it to the"). Zoltan called it boring, and it was.

What to build (own code, clean room): the rule-made plan uses the full preset, not only text templates:
- icons from the bundled sets (Lucide, Tabler, Iconoir) picked by keyword from each sentence;
- per-sentence layouts that change: big title, icon cards that pop in one by one, a numbered path or list for sequences ("first, then, next, finally"), a closing call to action;
- the preset's characters (for Stick World a stick figure that waves, walks and jumps);
- kinetic text with short, whole phrases; never cut a phrase in the middle of a sentence, shorten it to the key words instead;
- burned-in captions with the current word highlighted;
- gentle motion everywhere (floating shapes, slow camera push).
The two videos Claude made on 2026-11-18 (`C:/Users/gdp/Downloads/KH video ready Stickman/Hello Cambodia AI Coach v2.mp4` and `Hello Cambodia AI Coach 3D.mp4`) show the target level. Watch them for the idea only; build it in the app's own way.

## 6. A "Free only" switch

On the recipe and on the video: "Free only: never use a paid service". With it on, every paid step (AI scene plan, picture check, ElevenLabs music, HeyGen) is skipped without a question, the free path is used, and the Preview tab lists what was skipped.

## 7. Script tab: pasting a finished script

When the recipe's Start is "A pasted text is a finished script", the Script tab opens with the paste box visible and "Approve and continue" as the main button. "Write it myself" must not hide under More.

## 8. Voice: choose the provider per video

The voice list came only from the recipe's provider; to use his own ElevenLabs voice Zoltan had to change the recipe. On the Voice tab, let the user switch provider (Kokoro, ElevenLabs, HeyGen) for this video. The recipe stays the default.

## 9. Content language list

Show every language the app's providers can serve, with a note next to each one about which voice providers can speak it (see 3).

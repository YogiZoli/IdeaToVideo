# IdeaToVideo: install and first video

One page, about 15 minutes, Windows 10 or 11. You need one free key (Google
Gemini). Everything else has a free default.

## 1. Download

Open the Drive link you got and download `IdeaToVideo_0.1.0_..._x64-setup.exe`
(about 100 MB). If Google Drive says it cannot scan the file for viruses
because it is large, click **Download anyway**.

## 2. Windows SmartScreen

The installer is not signed yet, so Windows warns you once.

1. Double-click the file. A blue window says **Windows protected your PC**.
2. Click **More info**.
3. Click **Run anyway**.

No administrator password is needed: the app installs for your user only.

## 3. Install and start

Click **Next** and **Install** in the installer, then **Finish**. IdeaToVideo
starts. Later you find it in the Start menu.

On the first start a bar says **One more download before your first video**.
Click **Download now** (about 280 MB, once). You can carry on while it runs.

## 4. Setup: one free key, the rest skipped

The setup has a few short steps. Only the first one needs you.

1. **AI key:** keep **Gemini**. Click **Get a key**: Google AI Studio opens.
   Sign in with your Google account, click **Create API key**, copy it.
   Back in the app, paste it and click **Test the key**. It costs nothing.
   The key is stored only in your Windows keychain, never in a file.
2. Every other step: click **Skip, use default** (or **Use recommended**).
   You can change all of it later in **Settings**.
3. On the last step click **Finish**.

## 5. Your first video

1. Click **New ONE Video**.
2. Type a topic, for example *How to plan a calm week*. Click **Create ONE Video**.
3. Click **Finish on Autopilot** at the top. The app writes the script, makes
   the voice, plans the scenes and renders the video.

How long: the render takes about 2 minutes of computer time per minute of
video, a little more on an older laptop. While it runs:

- keep the app open (you can minimize it);
- do not let the computer go to sleep; keep a laptop plugged in.

While the script is written, the app also makes the YouTube **Packaging**:
title ideas, a summary and tags. You find it on the **Script** tab. Pick a
title or write your own, change anything you like, and click **Save**. If it
is missing, click **Make title, summary and tags (AI)** there.

## 6. Where your video is

When it is ready, the **Your video** tab plays it. Click **Show in folder** to
see the file. All videos are in your Documents folder, in
**IdeaToVideo Projects**, one folder per video.

## 7. Optional extras

You do not need these for the first video.

- **More photos (Unsplash):** **Settings**, **Identity**, **Footage**, then
  **Get a key** next to Unsplash. Make a free app on the Unsplash site and
  paste its **Access Key** (never the Secret key). A new key allows 50
  searches an hour. A free Pixabay key works the same way and also gives
  video clips. Then search for pictures on the **Scenes** tab.
- **Publishing connection:** only if we gave you a publishing address and a
  token. **Settings**, **Publishing**: paste both and click **Save**. Then
  **Send to Publishing** on the **Your video** tab sends the video's
  publishing card. Without it, **Export card** saves the same card as a file.

## 8. If something goes wrong

The error box tells you what to do in plain words. If it still does not work,
send us the diagnostics zip: see **How to send us your logs**
(`send-logs.md`, in the same Drive folder).

Thank you for testing!

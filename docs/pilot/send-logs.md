# How to send us your logs

Two minutes, Windows or Mac. Do this when something did not work, or when we
ask for it.

## 1. Make the zip

1. In IdeaToVideo, open **Settings** (top menu).
2. Click **Import & Export**.
3. At **Diagnostics**, click **Export**.
4. A save window opens. Save the zip on your Desktop, so you find it easily.

## 2. Send it to us

- **E-mail:** a new e-mail to us, attach the zip, and write one line about
  what you were doing when it went wrong.
- **WhatsApp:** open our chat, tap the paperclip (or **+**), choose
  **Document**, pick the zip from your Desktop, send. Add the same one line.

A screenshot of the error box helps too, if you still see it.

## What is in the zip, and what is not

In it: the app's log (what the app did and when, and any error codes), the
steps of each video (which step ran, how long, whether it worked), your
settings (without any key), and your
app version and system (Windows or macOS version).

Never in it: your keys, your voice recordings, your videos, or the text of
your scripts.
